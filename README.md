# NAMETY — сауни та чани

Public design prototype. Ukrainian copy, approved NAMETY fonts, original photographs.

## Pages

- `/saunas/`: sauna category.
- `/ofuro/`: hot tub category.
- `/saunas/sauna-for-couple/`: sauna for two.
- `/saunas/sauna-for-company/`: company sauna with its own tea room.
- `/saunas/wildsauna/`: wild sauna with a choice of rest room.
- `/ofuro/ofuro/`: ofuro tub with a choice of rest room.

The repository root mirrors the sauna category so the previously shared link remains useful. Navigation preserves the established NAMETY route names. All prototype pages are `noindex,follow`.

This is a buildless static site, hosted under `/namety-saunas-prototype/` on GitHub Pages. Fonts, styles, galleries and photos are local. The shared header/footer keep links to other approved NAMETY prototypes and the live booking service.

## Booking and content

Booking links open the real `sauna` or `tub` category. Room choices on the prototype update the displayed price and photograph; the final offer, room, date and time must be selected in booking. No availability is simulated and no reservation is submitted by the prototype.

The company sauna tea room, separate tea house and lounge are different spaces. Rates and capacities retain the approved prototype values: 2,100 / 3,000 / 2,500–3,000 UAH per hour; 2 / 8 / 6 / 4 guests. Minimum visit without overnight accommodation is two hours.

Two physical tubs are not represented as two invented products: the prototype uses the established `/ofuro/ofuro/` offer and its two rest-room variants.

Production `namety.ua` is not changed by this repository.
