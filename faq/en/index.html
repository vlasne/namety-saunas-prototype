Is prepayment required, and how can I pay?

Cabins, saunas and hot tubs require full prepayment. Partial payment for a cabin is possible only by agreement with the manager. You can pay by card using a payment link or by invoice; our team will provide the details.

Saunas and hot tubs

Can I book a sauna or hot tub without staying overnight?

Yes. You can book a sauna or hot tub separately from a cabin and visit just for your session.

What is the minimum sauna or hot-tub session?

The minimum is 2 hours for day guests and 1 hour for cabin guests. Check available times when booking.

Check session times
Link to this question
Is the hot tub private, and is it beside our cabin?

The booked hot tub is exclusively for your group during your session. It is in a separate spa area, not beside your cabin. Other guests may use the shared fire-pit area.

What should I bring to a sauna or hot-tub session?

Towels and sheets are included in spa sessions. Bathrobes are also provided with lounge-room packages; check what your chosen package includes with our team. Please bring your own rubber slippers.

No. Sauna and hot-tub sessions are booked and paid for separately from your stay.

Your stay

Can I bring a child?

Yes, children are welcome. There is no cot; suitable accommodation depends on the cabin and the child’s height. Before booking, tell our team the number of adults and your child’s age and height so we can help choose a suitable option.

Ask about accommodation
Link to this question
Can I bring a pet?

No. Pets are not allowed in the cabins or anywhere on the NAMETY grounds.

Contact our team
Link to this question
Are meals included, and where can we eat?

Meals are not included, and NAMETY has no on-site kitchen. Breakfast boxes can be preordered by 16:00 on your arrival day for delivery between 09:00 and 10:00. For lunch and dinner, order from partner restaurants using the QR code in your booklet. Ask our team about ordering.

Are the cabins warm in winter? What about power outages?

The cabins have air-conditioning units for heating and underfloor heating, with adjustable temperature. Before visiting, ask our team about current arrangements during power outages, which may affect electrical equipment.
