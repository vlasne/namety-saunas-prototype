Where can I see availability and the final price?

The online booking calendar shows available dates, session times and the full price. Select a service and dates to see the available options and the total to pay.

Availability and prices
How do I book a cabin, sauna or hot tub?

Open online booking and choose the type of visit. For a cabin stay, select your dates first, then choose from the available cabins.

Open online booking
Is prepayment required, and how can I pay?

Cabins, saunas and hot tubs require full prepayment.

By card
Using a payment link.
By invoice
By bank transfer using the invoice details.

Partial prepayment for a cabin must be agreed with the manager before the booking is confirmed.

Request an invoice by phone
When is my booking confirmed?

Your booking is confirmed after payment.

Booking held
For 24 hours from the time it is made.
Payment link
Valid for the same 24-hour period.
How do I change or cancel a booking?

To change or cancel a booking, call reception on +38 093 666 89 99.

Cabin
More than 7 days (168 hours) before check-in: a full refund. At 168 hours or less: the first night’s rate is charged.
Sauna or hot tub
More than 24 hours before the session: a full refund. At 24 hours or less: 50% of the session price is charged.

Charges are capped at the amount paid; the balance is refunded. Date changes follow these cancellation rules and require a new booking.

Is the hot tub private, and is it beside our cabin?

The booked hot tub is exclusively for your group during your session. It is in a separate spa area, not beside your cabin. Other guests may use the shared fire-pit area.

No. Sauna and hot-tub sessions are booked and paid for separately from your stay.

No. Pets are not allowed in the cabins or anywhere on the NAMETY grounds.
