The online booking calendar shows available dates, session times and the full price. Select a service and dates to see the available options and the total to pay.

How do I book a cabin, sauna or hot tub?

Open online booking and choose the type of visit. For a cabin stay, select your dates first, then choose from the available cabins.

Is prepayment required, and how can I pay?

Cabins, saunas and hot tubs require full prepayment. Pay by card using a payment link or by invoice. Partial prepayment for a cabin must be agreed with the manager before the booking is confirmed.

Request an invoice
Link to this question
When is my booking confirmed?

Your booking is confirmed after payment. Before payment, the booking is held for 24 hours from the time it is made; the payment link is valid for the same period.

Link to this question
How do I change or cancel a booking?

To change or cancel a booking, call reception on +38 093 666 89 99. A full refund applies more than 168 hours before cabin check-in or more than 24 hours before a sauna or hot-tub session. At or within these deadlines, the charge is the first night’s rate or 50% of the spa session, respectively, capped at the amount paid; the balance is refunded. Date changes follow the same cancellation and rebooking rules.

Is the hot tub private, and is it beside our cabin?

The booked hot tub is exclusively for your group during your session. It is in a separate spa area, not beside your cabin. Other guests may use the shared fire-pit area.

Your stay

Can I bring a child?

Yes, children are welcome. Every cabin has a 2 × 2 m double bed. Lito, Osin and Zyma also have a separate room with a non-folding sofa for a guest up to 160 cm tall. Cots are not provided.

Choose a cabin
Link to this question
Can I bring a pet?

No. Pets are not allowed in the cabins or anywhere on the NAMETY grounds.

Link to this question
Are meals included, and where can we eat?

Meals are charged separately; NAMETY has no on-site kitchen. A breakfast box for two costs UAH 1,000, with three options to choose from. Order and pay in full by 16:00 on your arrival day for cabin delivery between 09:00 and 10:00. Order lunch and dinner from Rozmaryn and Filleto restaurants using the QR code in your booklet. VDNG also has cafés and restaurants.

Are the cabins warm in winter? What about power outages?

Yes, the cabins are warm in winter. They have air-conditioning units for heating and underfloor heating, and you can adjust the temperature yourself. NAMETY has a generator for power outages. The saunas and hot tubs are wood-fired.

Help and safety

Where is the shelter, and what should I do during an air-raid alert?

The nearest shelters are in VDNG pavilions 1 and 5. They are open during air-raid alerts, including at night. We provide a booklet with the route at check-in. When an alert sounds, go to the shelter and follow official instructions.
